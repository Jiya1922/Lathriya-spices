Shipping Policy

Fast, Safe & Reliable Delivery Across India.

Order Processing

All orders are processed within 1–3 business days after successful payment confirmation. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays will be processed on the next working day.

Delivery Time

Delivery usually takes 3–7 business days depending on your location. Remote locations may require additional delivery time.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed during checkout before payment is completed. Free shipping may be offered during promotional campaigns.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive your tracking details through SMS, WhatsApp, or Email so you can monitor your shipment.

Delivery Delays

Delivery may occasionally be delayed due to weather conditions, natural disasters, public holidays, courier service issues, or other unforeseen circumstances.

Delivery Address

Customers are requested to provide a complete and accurate delivery address with a valid phone number to ensure smooth delivery.

Damaged Packages

If you receive a damaged package, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with photos of the package so we can assist you promptly.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding shipping or delivery, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
